## What’s in the README

### **Who it’s for:** 
Clinicians, epidemiologists, data analysts, and students. Each notebook follows your why → what → how teaching style.

### What you’ll learn:###
Seven areas:
- ECG physiology and leads
- noise
- filtering and signal quality
- QRS detection and waveform delineation
- HRV (time, frequency and nonlinear)
- synthetic ECG modelling and PCA/ICA
- machine learning; and a digital biomarkers section.
- That last one covers how wearable ECG compares with 12-lead, HRV as a biomarker, the V3 validation framework, model cards, and SaMD, EU MDR and the EU AI Act.

### Datasets: a table for UCI, BIDMC and PTB-XL.

### Roadmap: phases 1–2 are marked done, PTB-XL is in progress, and phases 4–8 are planned.

### Disclaimer: This repo is for “self learning and educational use only”.
